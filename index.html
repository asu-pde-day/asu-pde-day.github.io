Analysis and PDE Day at ASU

Nonlinear Structures, Waves, and Dynamics

A one-day, single-track workshop on nonlinear structures in partial differential equations and dynamical systems. The program brings together nonlinear waves, pattern formation, variational models, fluid dynamics, and dispersive hydrodynamics. The day combines invited lectures with a poster session for graduate students and postdoctoral researchers.

Join the interest list Present a poster

Hosted by the School of Mathematical and Statistical Sciences. Organizers: Donatella Danielli and Jimmie Adriazola.

Program

The day runs from about 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. as a single track of invited lectures, with breakfast, coffee breaks, a lunchtime poster session, and a closing open research exchange.

We will announce the invited speakers soon. Join the interest list to hear first.

Poster session and travel

Call for posters

The lunchtime poster session is open to graduate students and postdoctoral researchers working in analysis, PDE, dynamical systems, nonlinear waves, mathematical physics, and closely related computational or experimental problems. Tell us on the interest form that you plan to present, and we will collect titles and abstracts when registration opens.

Poster deadline: January 29, 2027

Travel awards

We are seeking funds for a limited number of travel awards for poster presenters, with priority for participants traveling from New Mexico and Southern California, then the broader region. If you would like to be considered, say so on the interest form. We will confirm the details once funding is in place.

Logistics

Venue

Getting here

Phoenix Sky Harbor (PHX) is about 15 minutes from campus by car, and the Valley Metro light rail runs from the airport (via the PHX Sky Train) to the Tempe campus.

Lodging

Hotels within walking distance of campus include the Tempe Mission Palms, Graduate Tempe, and Canopy by Hilton Tempe Downtown.

Weather

Late February in Tempe is mild, with highs near 70°F (21°C) and evenings around 50°F (10°C). Bring a light jacket for the morning.
